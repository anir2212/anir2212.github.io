EW Where it all started

The project that made me
want to build.

EasyBoard is where my interest in technology became a purpose: using it to make life better for the people around me.

For many people with Down syndrome, a bus ride is a long chain of decisions: which stop, which bus, when to press the bell. Each one is a chance to get lost, so a caregiver usually has to come along. My friend Shriniket Subramanian and I set out to change that through the Tech for Good Innovation Challenge, part of the Go Digital ASEAN initiative, with mentors from Google, Engineering Good, SG Enable and the Down Syndrome Association (Singapore).

We spoke with caregivers, the Down Syndrome Association and other stakeholders to understand what worried them about travelling alone. Turning those conversations into something that could genuinely make their days easier was eye-opening, and it has shaped what I've wanted to build ever since.

EasyBoard splits every trip into one-action steps, using bright colours, a voice that never switches off, and a button that calls a caregiver at any moment. It won the Judge's Choice Award and was featured in The Straits Times and in a news segment on CNA. The prototype below is what I'm building now.

The Straits Times · 3 June 2021 Students devise tech solutions to help people with disabilities Covers two student teams in the Tech for Good Innovation Challenge. It describes EasyBoard's simplified, brightly coloured interface, its emergency button for calling a caregiver and its always-on voice, and notes that user trials began in May 2021.     What's in the prototype

    • iOS and Android from a single Expo / React Native and TypeScript codebase, with separate traveller and caregiver modes
    • One engine for the steps, shared by the app and the server, with no dependencies: it builds the steps, tracks progress, gives walking directions and ranks routes
    • Its own turn-by-turn walking guidance on OneMap routes: a heading-up map, spoken turns, wrong-way detection and re-routing
    • Route recommendations that score each option on how simple it is, using live LTA bus waits, disruptions and the routes a caregiver usually picks
    • Built to be safe: Supabase in the Singapore region, row-level security tested with pgTAP, no API secrets in the app, and colours that meet WCAG AA contrast

    Expo · React Native · TypeScript · Supabase · Postgres · Deno · MapLibre · OneMap · LTA DataMall · Jest · pgTAP
